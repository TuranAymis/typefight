_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

TypeFight is a typing game. This page says what the game stores and why.

## What we store

- **Nickname.** You choose it. It is shown on leaderboards and to opponents. Do not use your real
  name if you do not want it shown.
- **Device token.** A random secret created when you pick a nickname. It proves to the game that
  the profile on this browser is yours. We keep only a hash of it on the server.
- **Game results.** Your typing runs and duels: speed, accuracy, who won, rating changes, badges and
  your chosen cursor effect.
- **Recovery code (optional).** If you create one, we keep only a hash of it.
- **Anonymous usage statistics.** A random id made in your browser (not linked to your nickname)
  and events such as "opened the game", "started a duel" and "finished a duel", with the day they
  happened. We use them to learn whether the game is used and kept. We do not store your IP address
  and no event contains your nickname or the text you type.
- **Settings.** Your sound on/off choice and whether you saw the tutorial.

Your nickname, device token, anonymous id and settings are saved in your browser (IndexedDB). The
game does not use cookies or localStorage.

## What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads of our own, or collect your name, email address, location or contact
details. Pages hosted by a game portal may use the portal's own cookies and ads under the portal's
policy.

## Do Not Track

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the game sends no usage statistics and
creates no anonymous id.

## Your choices

- Clear the site data for TypeFight in your browser to remove everything stored on your device.
- To remove your profile from our servers, contact us at **<your contact email>** with your nickname.
  Anonymous usage statistics cannot be linked to you, so they cannot be looked up individually.

## Children

The game is meant for a general audience and does not ask for personal details.
